# Competitive UX Review

This review covers public product/help pages for design patterns only. It is not a feature, performance, or pricing comparison, and does not establish that SWAPNO AI has equivalent functionality.

## References and patterns

- [Jasper Brand Voice](https://www.jasper.ai/brand-voice) and [Jasper IQ](https://www.jasper.ai/jasper-iq): reusable brand and product context can reduce repetitive setup. SWAPNO AI currently has Business Profile/Brain fields but no verified account AI generation using approved context.
- [Buffer Publish](https://buffer.com/publish) and [Buffer Help Center](https://support.buffer.com/): a clear time-based calendar aids review and planning. SWAPNO AI's calendar is a prototype; real scheduling/publishing is not connected.
- [Metricool Help Center](https://help.metricool.com/en/) and [planning](https://help.metricool.com/planning-z80uw): planned work should be distinguishable from measured results. SWAPNO AI account analytics and social connections are not live.
- [HubSpot getting started](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/get-started): task-based setup helps users learn without a forced long tour. SWAPNO AI has a skippable lesson/checklist implementation, but it lacks full account acceptance testing.

## Applied direction

- Center the authenticated workspace on a single Marketing Adviser.
- Collect business profile information gradually; let users review and approve it.
- Keep onboarding lessons replayable and social authorization optional.
- Distinguish plans/drafts from externally published content and measured results.
- Use honest empty states when connections or account data are absent.

## Limits

Public vendor pages can change and are not proof of competitor API terms, prices, or permissions. No competitor API or proprietary asset was copied or integrated. The more detailed initial review is retained at [docs/product/COMPETITIVE-UX-REVIEW.md](product/COMPETITIVE-UX-REVIEW.md).
